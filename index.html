// WikiDactic: Field Guide //

A living knowledge graph built from Wikipedia. Every article you explore becomes a node. Every link between articles becomes an edge. The more you learn, the bigger the map grows. Your progress is personal, tracked right here in your browser.

◈ How to Navigate
CLICK NODEOpen the article panel: read the article, study a curriculum, take a quiz
CLICK DOTFrontier nodes: small dim dots linked to known articles. Click to explore them
SEARCH ⌕Find any Wikipedia topic. Unclassified articles can be added to the graph
RANDOM ⚄Pull a completely random Wikipedia article and read it
DRAGGrab any node and reposition it. The layout is yours
SCROLLZoom in and out. Pan by dragging the background
HOVER EDGESee the relationship type between two connected articles
◈ Node States
Untouched: never opened
Viewed: article panel opened
Studied: completed the curriculum
Conquered: passed the quiz ★
